## 1. Method note

The report assumed LLM calls for scoring, clustering, drafting and fact-checking. Here each is a **file stage**: Claude writes `idea_candidate.json` / `evidence_packet.json` / `claim_ledger.json` against the fields below, a deterministic check validates required fields, enums and arithmetic (weights sum, ids resolve), and a **separate Claude verifier subagent** that receives only the artifact + the spec (not the producer's reasoning, Report §24.4) re-checks high-risk claims. No embedding/API service is required: do duplicate and cluster checks with canonical tags (species/topic/question type), title-pattern normalisation and the channel catalog; whether to add embeddings is an open question ([current-state-and-open-questions](current-state-and-open-questions.md)). The archived technews engine did have scorer/fact-checker prompts that called model APIs; do not restore that pattern ([agent-principles §1](../../../references/agent-principles.md)).

## 2. Topic graph (Report §4.3, §17.1)

The observed lane is **evergreen curiosity with topic-family adjacency**, not breaking news. Model topics as a graph (`ANIMAL/HUMAN BEHAVIOR` -> food taboo/non-adoption {species-specific, domestication constraint, disease/parasite constraint, economics/feed efficiency, culture/regulation}; predator behaviour {sleep, running, eye contact, perceived vulnerability}; domestication; animal cognition; human evolution/food choice).
After a winner, generate **horizontal** neighbours (predator meat -> shark/warthog/bear/crocodile) and **vertical** ones ("why not eat X" -> "why X was never domesticated" -> "what disease/biology blocked X" -> "what if X became livestock"). Each neighbour must pass its own evidence and novelty checks. Confidence: HIGH that the target repeats topic families, MEDIUM that a breakout caused it (Report §4.3) - an inference, so adjacency mining is a tactic, not a law.
Pipeline: `SOURCE -> INGEST -> NORMALIZE -> CLUSTER -> DEDUPLICATE -> SCORE -> VERIFY -> IDEA CANDIDATE` (§17.3). Idea signals and their proper use: search suggestions/related queries (phrasing, not proof of popularity); trend data (timing, not evidence); forums (misconceptions, not authority); scholarly/PubMed/Crossref-style search (can the script be sustained); government/institutional feeds (primary evidence + trigger); reputable news (trigger only); own analytics (strongest internal feedback) (§17.2). Competitor channel metadata: usable only through lawful/API access or manual observation - see [policy](policy-rights-and-risk.md); competitor data is never factual evidence.

## 3. Idea scoring (PROPOSED weights, Report §17.4 - calibrated to the observed lane, not TPDossier's real scoring)

```
relevance_to_current_lane 0.18 · curiosity_or_paradox_strength 0.17 · title_potential 0.13 · evidence_availability 0.13 · visual_potential 0.12
storytelling_potential 0.11 · novelty 0.08 · evergreen_value 0.05 · timeliness 0.02 · inverse_competition 0.01      (sum = 1.00)
```
**Hard vetoes** (override the score): evidence availability below threshold -> reject/backlog; title premise materially false -> reject; near-duplicate of the catalog without a new mechanism -> reject; the visual plan cannot communicate the core mechanism -> deprioritise; sensitive medical/safety topic beyond risk policy -> escalate to the owner.
**Curiosity/paradox rubric** (§17.5): 0.0 already obvious · 0.25 interesting fact, no unanswered conflict · 0.50 viewer can articulate "why?" from the title · 0.75 common observation contradicts an intuitive expectation · 1.00 contradiction is common, personally relevant, easily visualised, and supports a multi-layer explanation plus an exception/twist. Gate suggestion: take >= 0.75.
`idea_candidate.json` carries: angle, cluster/topic_family, score vector, evidence leads, probe status, packaging hypotheses (>= 3 title/thumbnail concepts - the handoff's packaging gate, Handoff §Gates 1), visual-plan feasibility, risks, adjacency relation.
Idea gate (Report §27.1): PASS if fits the mode, curiosity gap intelligible, evidence probe viable, catalog duplication acceptable, visual/story potential acceptable, title premise qualifiable truthfully; REVISE if the angle can be narrowed; FAIL if the premise is false/empty or evidence too thin.

## 4. Evidence availability probe (§17.6) - before full research

Require at least: one credible primary/institutional lead for the central mechanism (or a clear path to one); two independent credible sources for a broad causal synthesis not settled by one source; enough factual depth for >= 3 distinct explanatory beats; identifiable counterexamples/limitations. Result: `VIABLE` | `VIABLE_WITH_RISK` | `THIN` | `REJECT`.

## 5. Evidence packet and claim ledger (Report §18)

**Evidence packet must precede scripting**; no script starts from loose browser notes. Versioned `evidence_packet.json`: `packet_id`, `topic`, `research_cutoff`, `central_question`, `sources[]`, `claims[]`, `contradictions[]`, `unknowns[]`, `title_qualification{}`, `review_status` (`VERIFIED` required to script).
**Source hierarchy** (§18.2): (1) primary papers, official datasets, government/IGO docs; (2) systematic reviews/meta-analyses/high-quality reviews; (3) universities, professional societies, museum/zoo/wildlife institutions; (4) reputable secondary reporting that links to or accurately characterises primary evidence; (5) tertiary explainers for discovery only, never sole support for a contentious central claim. History: archival primary documents + scholarship; agriculture/economics numbers: official statistics, dated.
**Claim record** (§18.3): `claim_id`, `text`, `claim_type` (e.g. `CAUSAL`), `scope{species[], geography, period}`, `support[]` `{source_id, support_type, locator, confidence}`, `counterevidence[]`, `script_permissions[]` (e.g. `PARAPHRASE`, `NUMBER_WITH_DATE`), `risk`. `support_type`: `DIRECT` (source states/measures the claim in matching scope) · `SYNTHESIS` (reasoned across sources) · `EXAMPLE` (instance, not rule) · `CONTEXT` (background only) · `CONFLICTING`.
**Entailment check** (§18.4): compare each script claim with source scope - species/population, geography, period, sample size, causal vs correlational wording, lab vs field, risk vs certainty, number units/denominator, "can" vs "usually" vs "always". A citation is not valid because it shares keywords.
**Conflicts** (§18.5): record both in `contradictions[]`, weigh recency/method/scope, downgrade certainty, reflect material disagreement in the script, block absolute packaging if the disputed fact is central. Never "pick the more exciting source". **Quotes/names** (§18.6): exact source/locator or paraphrase without quotation marks; attribution must say what the researcher actually established; "first discovered" claims need especially strong provenance.
**Research-to-script interface** (§18.7): the writer receives central question, approved angle, claim ledger, source summaries, counterexamples, uncertainty notes, forbidden/unsupported claims, title qualification, desired causal ladder, target duration range - **not** raw web pages.
Evidence gate (§27.2): central claims sourced appropriately, causal scope verified, conflicts/unknowns recorded, key numbers/dates have provenance, title qualification supportable; **hard FAIL if the central explanatory mechanism is unsupported**. Roles: `researcher` builds, independent `fact_verifier` challenges using only ledger + sources (Report §24.2). Use the news-explainer habits for claim->source mapping as a model: [editorial-standards](../../news-explainer/references/editorial-standards.md).

## 6. Titles (Report §16)

Grammar properties: immediate comprehension without specialist words · a premise the viewer can challenge in one sentence · one conspicuous missing causal explanation · enough breadth for several mechanisms and a late correction (§16.2). Title families: see [format-and-script-grammar §2](format-and-script-grammar.md).
**Title safety record** (§16.3): `surface_title`, `literal_claim`, `defensible_claim`, `qualification_required`, `qualification_beat_id`, `evidence_status` (e.g. `SUPPORTED_WITH_SCOPE`), `misleading_risk` (0-1). **Reject if:** the title needs a false universal and the nuance destroys the premise; a key term changes meaning between title and script; evidence is too weak for the causal promise; it implies medical/dietary-safety conclusions not supported; it is a near-duplicate of an existing title without a new mechanism.
Also in the owner's wider rules: titles must not be deceptive packaging; see [policy](policy-rights-and-risk.md) and [qa-gates §14](../../../references/qa-gates.md).

## 7. Thumbnails (Report §15, Blueprint 15)

- **Evidence boundary**: target thumbnails were not retrieved; no claim about subject count, text length, typography or layout is observed (§15.1). Measurement (`thumbnail_forensic`: dimensions, OCR word count, subject boxes, saliency, contrast, annotation primitives, background complexity, title relation) requires reference images the owner is entitled to analyse - owner decision ([policy](policy-rights-and-risk.md)).
- **Original template families until measured** (PROPOSED, §15.3): `FORBIDDEN/ABSENT FOOD` (focal animal or meat/object + human/plate cue, visual contradiction with the title) · `A vs B CHOICE` (two comparable animals/foods, one selected/one rejected, shared scale) · `PREDATOR BEHAVIOR TENSION` (human and animal around one readable action or gaze line) · `MECHANISM REVEAL` (familiar subject + one unexpected internal/causal element, stripped background). Limit focal concepts; the thumbnail poses the question, the title frames it; never duplicate the whole title as thumbnail text.
- **Concept record** (§15.4): `concept_id`, `template_family`, `title_variant_id`, `focal_entities[]`, `relationship`, `text_overlay` (nullable), `curiosity_mechanism`, `asset_plan[]` (`reuse:`/`procedural:`), `risk_flags[]`. Generate 3-5 concepts; composite deterministic text/graphics; any raster focal subject generated separately; compose at thumbnail resolution only after concept QA.
- **Sizes/checks**: 1280x720; inspect at full size and ~168x94 ([rendering §8](../../../references/rendering.md)). The HES text rules (2-4 words, `?`, specific font) are that channel's own; adopt only by explicit channel decision.
- **Packaging gate** (§27.8): title and thumbnail complement rather than duplicate; one readable proposition; focal subjects legible small; title qualification does not make the packaging deceptive; no unsupported visual implication; no resemblance to a specific target thumbnail beyond generalised grammar; unique within your own catalog. Handoff adds a **provisional** gate early: >= 3 viable title/thumbnail pairs *before* full research - written here as a two-stage packaging gate (provisional after idea; final after QA) because the report's DAG puts final packaging after render and the handoff puts a packaging gate first.

## 8. Analytics feedback (Report §17.7, Blueprint 16) - design only; never built

Store outcomes by **idea feature**, not just video id: title family, topic family, animal/category, curiosity mechanism, thumbnail template, hook type, duration, number of mechanism layers, time-to-first-resolution, CTR, first-30 s retention, AVD/average percentage viewed, relative retention at each beat boundary, comments revealing misconceptions. Use shrinkage/sample-size-aware updating; never let one viral result permanently dominate the weights (Handoff §Agent instructions 10). Align retention timestamps to beat/shot boundaries. Honest scope: the repo's other channels have thin analytics too ([workflow.md Stage 10](../../../references/workflow.md)); ask the owner for exported numbers rather than assuming an API.
