## 1. The decision (user, 2026-10-11)

"Faceless but with a mask, or a bike helmet, or something else. The face cover changes from video to video with a hidden storyline linked to the script for each video, and the character must be as expressive as they can be only using movement and scene directorial work, not facial expressions." This overrides the earlier proposal to use the Blender Studio character Snow (PLAN-v2 decision 4) and STUDY A7 (faces) **for this character only**. A7's intent (readable emotion, eyes on the subject) is met through the body, the cover's direction and the camera. The "you" avatar appears in what-ifs (T1/T9) and in the bridge-to-today beat of history/crime Shorts. One fixed model and outfit (STUDY A8) is reused in every video; only the cover changes.

## 4. Body-language vocabulary (expression without a face)

1. **Head direction = gaze** (the cover's front is the eyes). Look first then turn: head leads, chest follows 3-5 frames later, hips last. Head snapping before body = surprise; drifting after = reluctance. 2. **Head tilt**: roll 5-15 deg = curiosity/confusion; pitch down = defeat/thinking; up and back = confidence/awe. 3. **Spine**: forward arch + closed chest = fear/shame/sneaking; straight + open = confidence; C-curve slump = defeat or relief (differ only in timing). 4. **Shoulders**: ears = tension; dropped = release; **bouncing 3-4 times at ~5 Hz, decaying = laughter**. 5. **Hands**: open palms = honesty/"I didn't do it"; fists = resolve; hand to cover = face-palm; fidgeting fingers = nerves. 6. **Weight**: back foot wary, front eager, shifting = impatience, locked still = shock. 7. **Timing**: anticipation >= 4 frames against the action sells intent; overshoot + settle sells weight; holds with breathing sell thought; the fastest change in a scene is the emotional peak. 8. **Idle amplitude is acting** (idle never stops, A4): calm = breathing 0.6 deg, sway 0.8 deg; nervous = 2x sway + finger fidget; shock = idle damped to 15 % for 0.6-1.0 s then a deep recovery breath. 9. **Camera and staging are part of the performance**: push in on a decision; low angle triumph, high angle defeat; wide + empty space = loneliness/awkwardness; a reaction cut to the environment (passengers turning) tells what "you" feels before "you" moves; visor reflections show what "you" looks at; silhouette against a bright window for the clearest read.

## 5. The 16 key poses (`lib/poses.py`, baked as pose-library Actions in `you/you_rig.blend`; angles body-relative: pitch + = forward/down, roll + = toward the character's left, yaw + = left)

1 neutral (upright, relaxed, even weight, calm idle) · 2 curious lean (upper spine 12-18 deg toward the subject, head roll 10-12 deg, one hand rises; ease-in 10-14 frames with a small double-take) · 3 confident (tall, chest up 5 deg, chin up 6 deg, hands on hips/loose fists, wide stance, low camera) · 4 scared recoil (spine curled back, pelvis back 6 cm, shoulders up 4-5 cm, forearms up palms out; 3-4-frame snap after 2 frames of anticipation) · 5 shock freeze (locked straight, head snapped to the cause, arms stop mid-gesture; arrive in 3-4 frames with overshoot, idle 15 %, hold 0.6-1.0 s, thaw on an exhale; cut to the cause and back) · 6 relief slump (C-curve 15-20 deg, pelvis -3 cm, arms limp; 4-frame inhale rise then a FAST 6-frame drop and long exhale) · 7 triumphant (arch 8 deg, V arms, anticipation crouch 6 frames, overshoot up) · 8 defeated (slump 20 deg, head down 30 deg, SLOW 20+-frame drop, no overshoot, minimal idle, high wide static-ish camera) · 9 thinking (hand at the cover's chin, finger taps every 1-2 s, slow orbit) · 10 sneaking (crouched 20 deg, knees 35 deg, scanning yaw every 0.5-1 s, toe steps) · 11 laughing (rocks 8 deg, shoulder bounces 5 Hz decaying over 1 s, hand to belly) · 12 awkward (hunch, half-turn away, rubs the back of the neck, fidget 2x, glance-away-glance-back) · 13 strain (plane door: lean back 15 deg, both arms gripping, elbows 30 deg, shake 1.5 mm at 12 Hz in the arms) · 14 flail (arched back, arms wide alternating, 6-8 frames per swing, quick handheld) · 15 shrug (shoulders up 4 cm, palms up; up 5 frames, hold 6, down 8) · 16 point (head arrives first, then the arm, index out).
STUDY A8's set maps to: freeze = 5, flail = 14, cheer = 7; sprint = UAL `Sprint_Loop`; fall = UAL `Death01`/`Hit_Knockback` cut on impact (H6); sip = a carried-prop helper (cup to the cover's mouth area, the cover stays on; the gag is that it can't drink).

## 6. Gates (`lib/gates.py`, all read the EVALUATED scene after `frame_set`, i.e. what renders; all must pass)

| Gate | Rule |
|---|---|
| `foot_slide` | planted feet drift <= **1.0 cm** (also split walk-only / acting-only) |
| `feet_on_floor` | soles within **1.5 cm** of the floor (CG-1 max 1.25) |
| `pops` | no isolated spike/jump: accel <= 1.5 cm/frame^2 limit with eased moves, rotation <= 30 deg/frame |
| `never_frozen` | motion in every **0.5 s** window (A4 "always alive") |
| `head_in_cover` | no head vertex outside the cover's fitted shape (static check per cover + in scene) |
| `penetration` (handle/set) | no body vertex > **1.0 cm** inside set/props (BVH, per-part 3-ray vote) |
| `hands_on_props` / `hand_*_on_handle` | grip point on the hand within **1.5 cm** of the prop's grip point on every frame of a hold; `hand_*_touches_handle` skin gap <= 0.6 cm |
| `seat_contact` | seated <= 1.0 cm into / <= 1.2 cm off the cushion |
| `cover_in_frame` | the cover is never cut by the frame (>= 2 % margin; CG-1 min 5.3 %) |
| `cover_unoccluded` | the cover is never hidden by the set (<= 2 % hidden; CG-1 0 %) |
| `plan_key_poses_ge_12` | >= 12 clean poses in the library (15 of 16 clean) with pairwise silhouette IoU **<= 0.90** (CG-1 max 0.849, relief_slump vs defeated) |
| A4 idle layer | breathing chest 0.6 deg (`spine_03`) + 0.35 deg (`spine_02`), shoulders 0.8 deg, period 3.6 s, never damped below 60 % (except the 15 % shock beat); weight shift every 4-8 s (seeded) pelvis 1.5 cm + 1.5 deg hip roll eased 1.2 s; head micro-motion yaw 1.2 / pitch 0.8 / roll 0.5 deg band-limited noise; blink n/a (faceless) |

In-scene reviews also check: the emotion beat reads at **4 fps on the contact sheet with captions off**; A1 never static; A2 located set; A3 environmental life; A5 anticipation/follow-through; A9 crowds (CG-1 had 28 passengers, 3 idle cycles, none closer than a mid-shot). Two lessons from the CG-1 final-render review: the overhead bin hid 30-43 % of the helmet and dead-straight arms on the walk were only found on the final, so both became every-frame gates (`cover_in_frame`, `cover_unoccluded`) and walks need an arm swing.

## 8. Hidden storyline ("UNIT 17"; purely visual, never said)

- **Premise:** "you" is a crash-test dummy that walked out of the lab (the fictional **Test Hall**) to meet the everyday systems it only knew through a windscreen at 50 km/h. It hides its face because a dummy's face gives it away (painted features, black-and-white calibration markers on the temples), so it borrows whatever cover the situation calls for. Warm, not dark: hide-and-seek; the "pursuer" is a kind lab Technician (clipboard, thermos, lost-pet energy) who misses it; the ending is a homecoming with permission to keep exploring. It licenses slapstick (the tell is a hollow plastic *tok*, never pain) and gives every cover a reason (always overprepared for the wrong danger).
- **Rules (the topic always wins):** (1) script first: each cover is justified by the video's own beat. (2) **Never in narration, captions, title, description or pinned comment.** (3) Budget: at most ONE active tell per Short, on screen <= 1.5 s total, never during the hook's first 2.5 s, the mechanism/x-ray window, or a beat about real people's harm (put it in a transition, reaction insert or end hold). (4) Passive glyph: one character per video, <= 4 % of the cover's height, in the left-temple slot, readable on pause, never pointed at/highlighted/orange. (5) No face before Phase 3 (near-reveals blocked by staging: back to camera, lightning white-out, steam, reflection; the balaclava guarantees no skin). (6) Tone: no capture-as-threat (no chases/weapons/authority), the Technician never runs and is never menacing, no injury ever. (7) No real people or brands: the Test Hall, Technician, dummy design and our own black/white quadrant marker disc (not a manufacturer's yellow/black) are fictional; no logos. (8) Fallback continuity: if a slot drops to no-character, its glyph moves to the next character Short (ledger `characters/STORYLINE-LEDGER.json`, rows with `status` planned/fallback). (9) In real-person Shorts "you" appears only in the modern bridge and never shares a shot with the stylised real person.
- **Glyphs:** the eight Phase-1 covers spell **marker (quadrant disc), U, N, I, T, 1, 7, costume** = "UNIT 17"; Oct 29's "1" is printed in ink with no brightener, invisible in normal light and dark against the glow under UV, and under UV the chin shows "PROPERTY OF THE TEST HALL" (if the UV claim isn't verified at stage R, glyph 1 moves on). Tells: one-motion seatbelt buckle with a crisp click (a dummy's reflex), the hollow *tok*, freezing at crash-test equipment, "LOST: TEST UNIT" flyer covered by a sticky note, the Technician's far-bank cameo (Oct 24), white van with a quadrant logo (Oct 26, Oct 30), a test dummy with the same markers + a "TEST HALL 2 km" sign (Oct 27), the Technician on the Halloween street and the porch light clicking off (Oct 31, the Phase-1 cliffhanger).
- **Reveal path:** Phase 1 hints Oct 21-31 (8 character Shorts) · Phase 2 pursuit in November (about 1 character Short a week; Technician closer each time and always just missing "you"; the flyer reappears; near-reveal about every 3rd video; souvenirs from past videos in the background) · Phase 3 the Test Hall (a crash-test/seatbelt/airbag what-if chosen by the user, **no earlier than about 6 weeks of hints**): "you" sneaks back, is found by the Technician, removes the cover **filmed from behind** (the reveal belongs to the Technician's reaction), is clipped a **FIELD TESTER** badge (permission to keep going); the full face is shown once, in a reflection, in the end hold · afterwards covers continue as "field-test gear" and the glyph slot lives on (season 2 can spell something new). The cover is never removed in any earlier video (the T9 punch ditches the rig instead).

## 10. Code layout and how to run

`characters/lib/` (pure-Python modules import without Blender): `motion` (easing, keyed parameter tracks, anticipation/overshoot/follow-through, deterministic noise) · `poses` (the 16 poses as rig-agnostic parameter dicts) · `idle` (procedural always-alive layer). Blender-side: `puppet` (pose solver: anatomical rotations, aim, two-bone IK with hinges measured from the UAL clips, fingers 0-1 curl, bake) · `avatar` (UBC build) · `avatar_mpfb` (MPFB2 build + retarget) · `covers` (swappable fitted covers) · `locomotion` (UAL in-place walks with calibrated root speed, CMU retarget, foot lock, procedural steps) · `acting` (`Performance`, grip) · `gates` · `cabin` (procedural narrow-body airliner cabin, reused by the plane-door Short and the Comet long) · `extras` (seated mannequin passengers: UAL mannequins, offset idle cycles, look-at layer). Other folders: `you/` (the built avatar `you_rig.blend`, pose library, `report*.json`, silhouette/pose sheets, `build_you*.py`), `cg1/` (the CG-1 scene `build_cg1.py`, `gate_report.py`, versioned `build_report_mpfb_v*.json`, diagnostics, `compose.py`), `timings.json` (wall times of every Blender job).
Hands for props: IK to a grip point with palm direction + finger curl (`puppet.py`); a held prop is static (door handle: hand follows) or carried (cup parented to the hand bone at the grip offset). Gloved MPFB2 hands per grip (pinch, knob, bar, draw, box) come from a per-video `hands_kit.py` (contact-fit in capsule glove frames -> `work/hands/hands.blend` + `hands.json`); `--capsule-hands` falls back.

Run Blender jobs with `python -I characters/run_blender.py <script.py> <log> [--blend file] [--mpfb] [--slot-in] [-- args]`: it takes the shared `BLENDER.lock` (FIFO, one Blender at a time, waits while ANY blender.exe runs, user jobs and calendar renders go first), appends wall time to `characters/timings.json`; `--mpfb` sets `BLENDER_USER_RESOURCES=<3Dassets>/tools/blender-user` for that process only (never the user's own Blender config); `--slot-in` = a `0x` ticket for jobs under 10 minutes (scene builds with gates, diagnostics, preview stills); normal tickets sort after every release-calendar ticket (`TICKET_DATE`).

## 11. `3Dassets/` library (grows per video; `manifest.json` = catalogue, `LICENSES.md` generated from it)

Layout: `characters/` (Rain and Snow from Blender Studio, **CC-BY credit mandatory**; Quaternius UBC bodies CC0 sharing the UE skeleton with the UAL clips; Kenney/Quaternius extras) · `animations/` (80 curated CMU mocap BVH clips with `index.json`, UAL 1/2 game clips) · `environments/` (Kenney city/industrial/factory/nature/pirate, Quaternius nature) · `props/` (64 Poly Haven realistic props, Kenney kits, Quaternius cars) · `textures/` (Poly Haven 2K, ambientCG 2K) · `hdri/` (2K studios, interiors, streets incl. `shanghai_bund` night, skies) · `tools/` (`fetch_assets.py`, `fetch_cmu.py`, `retarget.py`, `rhubarb/` lip-sync (parked: low priority, no talking faces), `proofs/`, `blender-user/` MPFB2). MPFB2 v2.0.18 is installed portable (GPL-3 extension; 11 CC0 asset packs); rigs: default, game_engine, mixamo, cmu_mb (CMU names = no bone map), openpose, Rigify; use only through `BLENDER_USER_RESOURCES` and `addon_utils.enable("bl_ext.user_default.mpfb")`.
Rules (user-approved 2026-10-11): allowed = CC0 first, CC-BY with the exact credit recorded, MIT/BSD/Apache tools, account-free downloads. **Never:** logins, account creation, purchases/paid tiers, piracy, NC/ND licences, "free" terms forbidding commercial YouTube use, generative-AI assets with unclear rights. Every addition goes through `fetch_assets.py` so the licence is recorded in the manifest (hand-copied assets get a manifest entry by hand + `fetch_assets.py licenses`). **Never open a library `.blend` and save it back; never edit files inside `3Dassets/` per video**: append/link into the production file (e.g. `bpy.data.libraries.load` the `CH-rain*` collections). Credit in the video description: copy the lines for the assets the video uses from the top of `LICENSES.md` (CC-BY Rain/Snow mandatory; Poly Haven and CMU request credit). The binary payload is gitignored; `manifest.json` + the fetch tools recreate the library on another machine. Blender 5.x gotcha: actions are layered (use `retarget.fcurves(action)`, set `action_slot`). Opening Rain/Snow with `--factory-startup` skips their rig UI scripts; the rigs still evaluate. Blocked Quaternius Google-Drive downloads were pending a retry (quota).

## 12. Production checklist for a "you" shot

1. Pick the beat's pose (section 5); key the **anticipation** (>= 4 frames, opposite direction) and the **overshoot/settle**. 2. Layer the idle at the beat's amplitude (calm/nervous/shock). 3. The head leads every change of attention. 4. Stage for the silhouette (no arms crossing the body against a same-value background). 5. Run all gates in section 6. 6. Read the 4 fps contact sheet with captions off: does the emotion read? 7. Check cover/frame/occlusion on the actual shot cameras, not just the pose sheet.
