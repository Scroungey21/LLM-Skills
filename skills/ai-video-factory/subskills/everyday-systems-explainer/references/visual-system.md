## 1. Principles (user requirement 2026-09-09, standing)

- Keep the visual language consistent: warm paper, dark structural lines, restrained functional colours, large objects, clear type, stable captions, native narration, physically meaningful movement.
- Vary the **working explanation** with the mechanism. A water barrier changes level and opens a gas path; electrical contact changes connectivity; a heat balance changes inputs and the system boundary. None is interchangeable with an icon sliding across the screen.
- **Every distinct core mechanism or sub-process the script depends on gets its own working animation.** "The compressor raises pressure", "refrigerant absorbs heat" and "the room receives heat plus work" are three explanatory tasks; a shared fridge outline does not cover them. If a sentence makes a sub-process central, implement it, show the relevant detail, or revise the script to claim only what the picture explains.
- **No nearest-template substitution.** Reuse a mechanism only if its represented components, connectivity, state variables and visible change match the explanation; otherwise extend with a verified state or build a new one. Text/evidence cards are for context-only qualifications, never a final replacement for a missing core mechanism.
- Reject a beautiful but wrong diagram; reject an accurate but unreadably tiny one; reject a lively but causally empty animation. Name the failed gate and fix it - do not average it away.
- Generated stills are allowed for natural subjects (birds/animals) after review; **diffusion-generated video, anatomical warping, sliding contact points and crude vector animals are never allowed**. Natural images do not become documentary evidence because they look photographic.
- Determinism: current time = `frame / fps`; scene time subtracts the measured scene start; rendering frame 200 directly equals rendering 0..200. No wall-clock timers, unseeded randomness, CSS time animation, accumulated browser state or unbaked simulation.
- One explicit state model drives related geometry (an aperture closes -> its flow path changes from the same parameter; water loses its seal -> the gas path changes at that threshold). Never animate labels and geometry from unrelated guessed delays.
- Separate **physical action** (the explanation), **representational cue** (arrows, particles; declare their meaning), **editorial motion** (camera). Transitions normalise `u = clamp((t - start)/duration, 0, 1)` onto a meaningful quantity; easing is editorial or a verified response, not a physics law; no bounce on every valve. A state change (tripping, sealing, contact) must not loop automatically while the narration says it stays changed: freeze the result long enough to read; never reset at the next scene unless explained (carry the state, or cut to a labelled comparison).
- Motion timing follows measured word timings; a transition too long for its narration is redesigned, never truncated, and narration is never stretched to rescue a visual.

Fonts: Montserrat Bold/ExtraBold/Black (SIL OFL 1.1, downloaded with approval), embedded as bytes in `fonts-data.ts` so no frame waits on a font request; rendering is held until fonts load and a load failure cancels the render instead of falling back.

**Colours.** `PALETTE`: ink `#132D39`, paper `#FCF7EB`, teal `#16A6AA`, orange `#EB673D` (a *part* colour only, no UI element uses it), gold `#E7AE3C`, mist `#DAD8CD`, water `#9ED8D6`, steam `#EEF6F5`, metal `#B9C2C4`, steel `#7C8B90`, red `#D2452F`, rubber `#3B4A50`, wood `#C99A62`, glass `#CFEAF0`, hot `#F2A65A`, cold `#7FC6E4`. Callouts: ink pill with paper text. Short CTA pill: signal yellow `#FFC400` with ink text. No UI orange or cream-navy-orange triad in text/UI (reads as Claude's brand colours; 2026-10-05).

**One highlight look (2026-10-03, colour restored 2026-10-10):** the lit part is always the same bright orange whatever its own colour or size: fill tinted 62% toward `#FF8A3D`, outline `#B8460E`, glow `#EB673D`, only a faint trace of texture. A lit line/wire/spring (no fill) = orange core inside a deep-orange casing. Everything else dimmed only by a **38% wash toward paper** (`DIM_WASH`). `signal=True` on a part whose colour is the information (signal lamp red/yellow/green, indicator LED): when lit it gets the orange outline but keeps its fill (a green lamp never reads olive; same in the x-ray look). Pointer arrows and callouts use deep orange `ACCENT #B8460E`, never the bright fill, so they never read as a second lit part. Yellow is only for text (thumbnail key word `#FFD21F`). A 2-day signal-yellow trial (2026-10-05) was reverted ("the outline should stay in orange"). Env overrides `REMOTION_HES_HIGHLIGHT*` exist for experiments only.

**Camera.** `DRIFT=.06` push-in across a run of shots that keep the framing (never restarts per step); a new framing is one direct move from where the camera really is (`TRANSITION_MS=900`); a switch to another diagram is a straight cut; a scene that opens in the state the previous scene ended in continues that state's clock (`sceneSegments` `carry`): what happened stays happened (a removed panel must not come back and be removed again). The authored side: `fit(diagram, ids, "short", state)`; no A>B>A excursions.

**Thumbnail composition** (`GhostThumbnail3`, 1280x720): see `metadata-and-thumbnails.md`.

## 3. The v3 diagram DSL (`authoring_v3.py`)

- `Diagram(id, title, view=(0,0,1600,900), kind=None|"exterior", references=[(src, shows)], scale="1 unit = 5 mm")`. Long shows the whole view at zoom 1 (~1476x830 px on screen): fill it; make the mechanism big enough that zoom 1 reads on a phone.
- Part builders: `rect fluid circle ellipse line path polygon polyline spring gear arrow label group` (group is a context manager; children move/rotate together; `ox, oy` pivot). Common kwargs: `fill stroke sw`, `material`, `say=[aliases]`, `only=[states]`, `flow` + `dash=[on,off]` for streams, `signal`, `free="<why apart>"`, `structure=True`, `clip="<host id>"`, `detached="<why>"`, `free_flow="<why>"`.
- States: `d.state(name, duration, loop=True, extends=None)`, then `.track(part, prop, keys, clock="global", period=...)`, `.to(part, prop, start, end, t0, t1, ease)`, `.cycle(part, prop, a, b)` (a>b>a, seamless loop), `.spin(part, turns)` (whole turns for seamless loops), `.pulse`, `.flow(part, speed)`, `.hide(*parts)`. Eases: `linear in out inout step`. `clock="global"` samples keys at video time modulo `period` so machinery keeps running through state/camera changes.
- Scenes: `P.scene(variant, id, role=, chapter=, headline=, narration=, claims=, visual=visual(diagram, state, before, change, after, camera=, highlight=, focus=, point=, callouts=, steps=[step(cue_word, state=, camera=, highlight=, ...)]), summarizes=, core=)`. Cue words must occur in order in the narration and not be among the first two words. `callout(text<=28 chars, x, y, to=)`.
- `Diagram.feed(stream, (part, prop, min), ...)`, `Diagram.teardown(...)` (see `editorial-and-quality.md`), `kinematics_v3.Loop.through([...], radii=[...], closed=True).follow(...)` for closed paths (straight runs + fillet arcs; every part rides the same loop at one speed; loop time = time to return to the start).
- `P.materials([(regex, material)])`, `P.structures([regex])`, `P.sound(...)`, `P.music(id)`, `P.term(...)`, `P.thumbnail(text, diagram, state, accent_word=, camera=cam(...,zoom 1.8-2.4), highlight=[...], photo=True, place=(x,y,zoom), dim=.62, saturate=.85)`.
- Labels: >=30 world units, <=32 characters, never overlapping other labels at zoom 1; partially counter-scale when zoomed, fade near the frame edge; not drawn in thumbnails.
- Loops must be seamless (rotate gears by whole tooth pitches; cycle a>b>a). Change something every 3-5 s (3.5: 3.5 s long / 2.5 s Short) with `steps` on cue words.
- Overlay cards (`question/stat/compare/list`) are accepted by the schema but never drawn.

## 4. Physics, attachment and framing gates

Attachment (`attachment.py`, user rule 2026-09-27 after the piano's music desk floated 42 mm above the case and the watch crown's grip lines slid off the crown). Rebuilds every part's outline (motion + group transforms applied) at the start (and mid-loop) of each state a video shows:
- every drawn part must touch, overlap or sit inside the rest of the object, unless `free="<why>"` (spray, falling scraps, molecule in open space, end-on cross-section, dimension lines, a layer lifting away in an exploded view). Off-canvas or clipped parts don't count. The fix is almost always a missing real support (bracket, shaft end, case back, foot), never a nudge.
- **One structure is one piece:** a casting/moulding/machined part drawn in several pieces goes in a group with `structure=True` (or `P.structures([...])`): every outline first, every fill over them, one shading and one glow; the group must be one connected piece. Merely attached parts (a knob on a panel) stay separate.
- Only the highlighted part glows (children no longer glow individually).
- Details that move over a part (grip lines as a crown turns, embossing, vanes on a spinning disc, clothes against a drum wall) go in a group with `clip="<host id>"`: masked by the host silhouette with the host outline redrawn on top; fails any detail leaving its host by >10% while moving.
- `validate_continuity`: a scene may not open in an earlier teardown step than already reached.

Physics (`motion_check.py`, revision >=3.5; user caught a toilet stream with no water behind it and zipper teeth spinning/popping/sliced):
- `check_fed`: every part that flows declares what feeds it via `Diagram.feed(stream, (part, prop, min), ...)` (tank `level`, pump state, valve opening); the renderer fades the stream the moment any source drops below `min`. A stream that never stops says why: `free_flow="river"`. Conveyor dashes (chains/belts) count as streams: feed from the motor or give a reason.
- `check_connected` (2026-10-04): a stream fed by a liquid must be DRAWN touching that liquid, directly or through other drawn liquid (the toilet bent pipe touched the bowl only above the water line; the rising leg from the outlet was missing). Non-liquid carried from the source (steam over a lid, heat through a wall, air pulled in by draining water, soap already falling) says `detached="<reason>"`.
- `check_spin`: unwrap computed rotation keys so each is within 180 degrees of the previous; fails a half turn or more between keys <0.25 s apart (a 0 > -360 jump spins a part a full turn in two frames).
- `check_wraps`: a part on a global-clock loop jumps back once per period; invisible only where another part takes its exact place. Otherwise the wrap must happen outside every camera showing the state: extend the row past the frame or hide the wrap under a cover.
- `check_canvas`: a floor/tape/pipe/row drawn to the canvas edge must not end in a straight cut in view: extend it past every camera or keep the camera inside the canvas.
- `check_camera_return`: see rhythm rules.
- `validate_motion`: no part fades out and back in while travelling along a loop (`op` keys min <=.15 and max >=.5 on a moving part).

Framing (`validate_framing`, `diagram_geometry`): every camera move checked per variant on its real stage: subject (highlight+focus) >=90% in frame (or covering >=50% of the view), clear of the Short rail; callouts >=98% in frame; label boxes (>=60% visible) and callouts must not overlap at on-screen size; a Short shot with no `highlight/focus` fails.

## 5. Materials and textured bookends (revision >=3.4)

Opening scenes (hook, promise) and closing ones (payoff, tease, endscreen; in the Short payoff and cta) render with real materials; the explaining scenes between are the flat drawing; 1.4 s crossfade (`pipeline_v3.texture_plan`). Materials come from `visual-system/v3/materials.json`; tiles are generated by `visual-system/v3/make_textures.py`, sized in millimetres and fixed to each part. Names: alu-cast, alu-dark, alu-plate, alu-brushed, stainless, stainless-incline, chrome, steel-oiled, steel-machined, steel-painted, steel-blued, cast-iron, brass, copper, motor-paint, rubber, friction-pad, concrete, void, glass, ruby, wood-spruce, wood-hard, wood-dark, felt, enamel-white, paint-light, plastic, paint, fabric, water, steel-tint, flat. Coloured plastic/paint/fabric: use `plastic|paint|fabric` over the part's own fill, so set the real colour. `flat` only for drawn aids (annotation lines, motion markers, lamps). `validate_materials` fails any part without a material in a diagram shown in those scenes; a `P.materials` rule matching nothing is an error (typo guard); choose by what the part is really made of (from your reference photos).

## 6. Translate a script into an animation plan

Do this before geometry (v2 stored it as `examples/<slug>/animation-plan.md`; v3 topics keep the equivalent in the topic file and REFERENCE.md):
1. Read the whole script and sources; name the actual device variant, system boundary and exclusions.
2. Break each scene into claims and causal verbs (opens, closes, bends, rotates, flows, heats, evaporates, compresses, blocks, senses, switches, releases, equalises).
3. For each verb name the physical entity and the state variable that changes; if neither can be named the sentence may be description, not explanation.
4. Draw the connectivity/containment map: what touches, contains, joins, is separated; where material or energy can go.
5. State initial condition, trigger, transition, observable result; include an unchanged variable so the counterfactual is interpretable.
6. Choose the view that exposes the transition (section, contact detail, energy diagram, assembly, close-up, comparison).
7. Match each visual event to words in native timing (anticipate a prediction, coincide with an action verb, or follow as payoff).
8. Check the silent storyboard (can the viewer see what changed and why?), then against the spoken explanation.

Plan template (one row per explanatory beat, not per video). Sections: *System and evidence* (representative variant; boundary and exclusions; references and claim ids; what is literal vs schematic vs time-compressed; viewer question and visible answer) · *Coverage inventory* (table: spoken clause/scene, mechanism, source/claim, reuse/extend/new, component+state, missing work; context-only qualifications may use a card, an unimplemented core process may not) · *Physical model per mechanism* (entities, connections, containment, contact points; moving parts, axes/pivots; state variables and units; initial state; trigger and transition; terminal state; what stays unchanged; allowed paths for material/energy/current/signal and how they differ; conditions where the representation stops being valid; which quantities are illustrative; invariant checks that expose a wrong animation) · *Scene and speech timing* (beat, native cue word, before, physical change, after, camera reason, label/caption space, transition/hold timing; continuity across scene boundaries; any transition too long for its narration) · *Natural assets* (source/rights or generation provenance, hash, aspect, placement, contact anchors, permitted motion, actual composite review) · *Verification record* (silent proof frames; initial/transition/terminal checks; direct-frame and backward-seek determinism; phone-size readability; caption/event alignment; claim vs visible behaviour; deliverable paths; audio measurements and listening status; rejected versions and the defect corrected; remaining limitations kept separate from completed work). Closing question: does every core explanatory clause have a visible, source-consistent working mechanism? If not, list the remaining work instead of declaring completion.

Delivery sequence: plan > reuse the approved shell and build/extend the mechanism with one state model > add typed support/validation > author scenes with precise before/change/after and claim ids > short silent proof (initial, transition, result frames) before narration cost > align to native speech and inspect frame zero, every major event, every scene boundary and the payoff in both aspect ratios > validate + normal render + check real MP4s and thumbnails > deliver component, package, plan, source ledger, asset provenance, videos, contact sheets and an honest QA note. Minimum new-mechanism verification: correct initial state; trigger causes the right transition; no impossible contacts/flow/containment; terminal state persists; direct-frame and backward-seek render deterministically; a short scene cannot hide the outcome; labels/captions legible; changing an unrelated parameter does not break anchoring. Tests target these failure cases, not a function returning the hard-coded number in its own implementation.

## 7. Worked references (v2 components; read the source before reusing)

- **Water barrier** (`TrapScene.tsx`): one centreline for outer pipe, lumen and mask: `M230 180V525Q230 670 385 670Q540 670 540 525V390H865`; outer stroke 132 units, lumen 110; water clipped to the lumen (a free blue rectangle falsely implies water outside the pipe); screen y grows downward. Levels: wet 445, dry 760, drying `min(760, 445+80t)`, refill `max(445, 760-90t)`; the gas path connects when level >615. These are drawing coordinates and illustrative rates (a time-compression label stops a few seconds reading as real evaporation). Quality = coupled change: full water blocks, falling water removes the barrier, the orange gas path connects, refill restores. Never run a gas particle stream through blue water; distinguish gas direction from wastewater direction. Checks: wet, just before seal loss, just after, fully dry, mid-refill, restored. A siphon/vent explanation needs its own sourced air-path and pressure model; evaporation does not explain siphoning.
- **Heat balance** (`HeatScene.tsx`): kitchen boundary containing cold cabinet, hot side and work input; `work/release/balance/outside` activate the electrical contribution and the larger outgoing heat cue; `balance` adds a same-room return; `outside` adds a destination boundary. Orange/teal movement is a schematic energy cue, not refrigerant particles. Keep material circulation vs energy transfer distinct; pressure/phase explanations need a sourced local thermodynamic view with labelled phases; no unsourced pressures, temperatures or efficiencies. Do not stretch the single wavy coil to every component.
- **Natural bird + contact diagram** (`NaturalBird.tsx`): reviewed raster still, image box x80/y155/780x520, foot anchors [425,622] and [585,622], contact [790,236] in the 1000x940 diagram space (valid for that asset in that placement only). Body rigid, feet stay on the conductor; the schematic hardware moves for the counterfactual; current-path overlay shown at completed contact, absent at larger separation; both feet share wire height. Never detach the head, rotate a flattened wing, shear the body, or imply wildlife behaviour. Inspect the actual paper composite for halos, clipping, stray background, feet penetrating the wire. Natural-image provenance fields: `id, subject_kind:"natural", media_type:"generated-still"|"photograph", source_path, path, sha256, rights_basis, provenance, review_status:"visually-reviewed", motion:"anchored-still", anchors`; a generated still is not a real photograph (the real-photo thumbnail requirement still needs an actual licensed photo).

## 8. Mechanism-specific variation logic

Design assignments (research the chosen design first; these are not verified models): valve (show closure element, travel/pivot, seat, changing passage; flow coupled to opening; reject a bobbing valve icon beside an arrow) · bimetal strip tripping a latch (strip geometry, bend, engagement, released contact; qualify time scale) · solenoid armature (coil, armature travel, linkage, energised vs released) · meshed gears (fixed tooth engagement and axes; verify relative rotation; reject two independently spinning gears) · pump (the actual pump type: chamber/rotor/valve behaviour; reject a universal spinning impeller) · sensor to controller (separate sensing, signal, decision, actuator; reject a blinking dot) · meter (model the selected principle; distinguish measured input from display update) · roof vent (air connection and pressure relationship; reject evaporation + a roof icon) · elevator choosing a car (calls, car states, an explicit policy; reject arbitrary motion as a universal algorithm).

Pick the animation grammar from the explanation: hidden interior (exterior > section > moving internal part > exterior consequence) · invisible flow (boundary > quantity > valid path > change from a restriction/input/destination) · failure (functioning > one changed condition > failure mechanism > repair) · counterfactual (fixed subject and framing, change one condition) · assembly (preserve relative locations, separate only needed parts, show coupling, reassemble) · control system (input > sensing > decision > actuation > feedback in causal order) · scale change (establish location > zoom to the level > explain > reconnect). Use multiple views when one diagram cannot carry it. Every camera move answers "what becomes easier to understand from here?" and every animated element "what does this movement mean?" - remove motion with no answer. An episode can combine modules, preserving where each part belongs: whole device > detail > action > consequence in the larger system; do not make every scene a disconnected poster.

The twelve Sep 13-18 mechanisms (`components/EverydayMechanisms.tsx`; coupling and rejection checks):
- Zipper: Y slider joins two rows locally, separate enlarged interlock for sideways load, reversal separates at the slider; teeth outside the slider keep their prior state; mating shapes must capture one another; never join the whole length at once.
- Pen cam: axial button travel, deliberately unwrapped rotary track (horizontal follower = rotation, labelled), shallow stop and deep return; `click` compound state for the Short; barrel fixed; tip outside at the shallow stop, inside after deep return.
- Door closer: piston/rack displacement compresses a spring and rotates a pinion; oil bypass through a restriction; rack/pinion phase and spring length follow one displacement; oil reverses with motion; spring force and flow resistance have different roles.
- Soap pump: fixed chamber, moving hollow piston stem, inlet/outlet check valves; dispense shrinks the chamber with inlet shut/outlet open, return expands it with valves reversed; never liquid through a solid piston or both valves open.
- Seatbelt: vehicle-sensitive inertia ball lifts a sensing linkage and moves a pawl to the reel; pawl approaches the real tooth path, reel stops after contact; the ball is a trigger, not the load path; not the webbing sensor/pretensioner/load limiter.
- Vacuum flask: double wall, declining gas, low-emissivity wall, radiation paths, residual neck/lid bridges, reversed heat for cold contents; keep a residual heat path; never claim vacuum removes radiation or all losses; particle count/arrow size qualitative.
- Can opener: enlarged rim between traction and cutting wheels; grip advances marked metal past the cut; slip rotates without feeding; both contact tangents follow feed direction.
- Stapler: driver pushes a U staple, anvil grooves bend legs inward below the paper, crown stays above; driver retracts, staple stays; legs reach the anvil before bending.
- Toaster: carriage loads a spring; electromagnet holds the latch; heating element distinguished from holding coil; coil switches off before release; timer triggers release but is not the lifting force.
- Wiper linkage: continuous crank drives a rigid coupler and rocker; `wiperPoints(t)` solves the circle intersection (A=(250,640), D=(665,640), crank 85, coupler 385, rocker 190); never interpolate joints separately; tests sample 1,000 states for constant lengths and output reversal; global time keeps continuity across cuts.
- Combination lock: exploded wheel pack, gates aligned or blocked; fence enters before the release linkage moves; a solid rim blocks entry; all gates align before the fence drops.
- Sprinkler: fixed body with pressure chamber, moving riser/flange, return spring, central nozzle feed; low pressure gives incomplete extension; pressurised chamber below the flange, spring between housing and flange; do not move the buried body.
For all twelve: schematic dimensions and durations are for explanation, not CAD; most transitions occur once and hold their end state; a Short preserves every physical step its own script names (the pen Short needed its own compound state).

## 10. Acceptance rubric (all must be true)

A silent viewer can point to the changing part or relationship · the picture shows the cause and consequence the narration asserts · each distinct core mechanism has implemented behaviour (no card or nearby template stands in) · geometry respects the real system's connectivity, containment, pivots and contacts · schematic quantities and compressed time cannot be mistaken for sourced measurements · subject and active change readable on a phone (text never repairs an incomprehensible picture) · natural anatomy plausible and contact-stable, no diffusion video · animation follows native narration and reaches a readable payoff · actual rendered files pass visual review and objective checks, with human listening reported separately if unavailable. Never report completion while a core mechanism is a generic icon, text card or decorative movement; report precisely what was implemented, checked and still unverified. Do not upload or schedule without owner authorisation.
